# Project Brief: BetterMe

*Draft v1 · Built as a product for the Qubators bootcamp*

## Overview

BetterMe is a free app that helps students prepare for **WAEC, TOEFL, SAT, and GRE**. Students find out where they are weak, practice under exam conditions, and get help whenever they are stuck.

## The Problem (proposed)

Students often study without knowing their weak topics, rarely practice under real exam conditions, get little explanation when they get answers wrong, and may have unreliable or costly internet.

## The Solution

Students pick their exam and take a short adaptive diagnostic test. It shows a topic-by-topic breakdown of their strengths and weaknesses. From there they practice specific topics under a timer and read the explanation for every question. They can also read short topic notes and take full-length mock exams. A personalized AI tutor, a community of other students, and a weekly study plan support them along the way.

## Key Features

- Exam selection, with the ability to switch exams while keeping each exam's progress separate
- Adaptive diagnostic test with a full topic breakdown
- Timed practice, with an explanation for every question and a "Report this question" button
- Full mock exams that show progress compared with earlier mocks
- Personalized AI tutor
- Automatically updating weekly study plan, plus reminders and badges
- Community Q&A and a leaderboard
- Offline practice and low data use

## First Version Scope

Multiple-choice questions only. Completely free for every student.

## Success Looks Like (proposed)

Students complete the diagnostic, practice regularly, and improve on their weak topics and across mock exams.

## Next Step
