# Exam Prep Companion App: Feature List

A students' companion app for preparing for **WAEC, TOEFL, SAT, and GRE**. Built as a product for the Qubators bootcamp.

---

## 1. Accounts and Exams

- Students sign up and log in, then select which exam they are preparing for (WAEC, TOEFL, SAT, or GRE)
- Students can switch between exams at any time, and each exam's progress is saved separately
- The exam date is optional
- The target score or grade is optional
- The app is completely free, with everything open to every student
- The first version focuses on multiple-choice questions only

## 2. Diagnostic Test

- It is the first thing students see after picking an exam
- It is a short test that finds the student's current level and weak areas
- It is taken in one sitting by default, with the option to pause and continue later
- Question difficulty adapts to the student
- Results show a breakdown of every topic across all subjects and categories

## 3. Practice

- Tapping a topic in the results breakdown goes straight to practice questions on that topic
- Practice is timed to simulate real exam pressure
- Difficulty is adaptive: questions get harder as the student improves and easier when they struggle
- After each test, the student gets a total breakdown of the quiz
- Every question has a written explanation, especially the ones the student got wrong
- Every question has a **"Report this question"** button so students can flag mistakes or confusing wording

## 4. Topic Notes

- Every topic has short written notes covering key ideas and formulas
- Students can read them before or after practice

## 5. Full Mock Exams

- Each exam has a full-length mock paper
- Results include a comparison with the student's previous mocks, so they can see whether they are improving

## 6. Progress and Planning

- A progress screen shows the student's improvement per topic across many quizzes
- A weekly study plan sets goals, such as "This week: fix your three weakest topics"
- The weekly plan updates automatically as the student improves or falls behind
- Reminders nudge students to study at a time they choose
- Badges and rewards mark milestones, such as finishing a topic or reaching a score

## 7. Home Screen

- A **"Continue where you left off"** button
- A shortcut to the AI tutor
- A summary of recent progress, such as the latest quiz score or the topic that improved most
- Badges and reminder status
- A countdown to the exam date, shown only if the student entered one
- A link to the notes for the student's weakest topic

## 8. AI Tutor

- The tutor is personalized: it knows the student's weak topics, quiz history, and exam date
- It suggests what the student should practice next
- Students can ask questions in plain language, such as "explain this again in a simpler way"
- Access points:
  - An **"Ask the tutor about this question"** button inside quiz explanations
  - Its own chat screen for any question at any time
  - A shortcut on the home screen

## 9. Community

- The community is open to everyone, as one shared community with topics and exams as tags or filters
- Students can post a question about a topic and get answers from other students
- A leaderboard ranks students by quiz scores

## 10. Offline and Low Data

- Students can download quizzes and lessons to practice offline, and their results sync later
- The app is kept light so it uses very little data
